How to play
  1. Read the tickets on the rail. The bar under each one is the customer's patience.
  2. Grab food from the crates. Chop or roll it on a board. Grill patties or bake pizzas, and pull them out before they burn.
  3. Build the order on a plate or tray and serve it at the pass. Fast service earns bigger tips.
